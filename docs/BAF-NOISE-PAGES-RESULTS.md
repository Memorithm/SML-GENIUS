Pre-registration: `docs/BAF-NOISE-PAGES-PREREG.md`, merged in #719
(e6bc86fd, 2026-10-25 17:16 UTC) **before** these runs. All 24 configured
runs (3 searches × 4 noise rates × 2 editable-page counts) were run once with
the fixed grid; all are reported; TEST/OOD were inspected only after all
24 finished. Method study at width 7 only, no learnability claim.

Command per configuration:
`--width 7 --seeds 8 --max-degree 3 --parity4 --max-quad-flips 70 --max-evals 5000 --search S --label-noise P --editable-pages N`.

Shown is the arm with sufficient degree per family. `train` is measured
against the *noisy* TRAIN labels; val/test/ood are clean. Status: 0 ok,
1 budget / term-limit fail-closed. Inactive baseline val/test/ood:
conj2 5.50/4.00/5.00, xor2 12.25/9.88/8.00, parity3 11.75/10.62/8.00,
parity4 11.38/10.12/8.00, and3 2.00/1.88/3.25, random 11.38/9.00/7.00.

## Hypotheses vs outcome

- **H1 (kflip noise robustness): supported.** At 2 pages, kflip keeps
  clean val/test/ood at 0 on conj2/xor2/parity3/parity4 up to P = 0.10, and
  at P = 0.20 stays far below baseline (val ≤ 2.0). and3 degrades slightly
  (val 0.62 → 1.50) but stays below baseline 2.00.
- **H2 (gf2 under noise): supported, stronger than predicted.** gf2 admits
  0/8 on every parity family and conj2 at every P > 0 (inconsistent system
  → baseline), and and3 drops to 2/8, 1/8, 0/8. Honest failure, no degraded
  fit admitted.
- **H3 (more pages): refuted for kflip.** With 4 editable pages the kflip
  preflight exceeds the fixed 5000-evaluation budget, so every kflip fit
  fails closed (status 1, 8/8, all families). Beam at 4 pages fits conj2 at
  degree 2 (8/8) but fails closed at degree 3 (and3, random), as predicted.
  gf2 is unaffected by the page count (exact at P = 0). Per protocol, no
  rerun with a larger budget.
- **H4 (random null): consistent with null.** No configuration is
  meaningfully below baseline on random TEST/OOD (best: beam P = 0.2 OOD
  6.25 vs 7.00, while kflip OOD 8.25 > 7.00). Seed-noise threshold was not
  quantified in the prereg; no claim is made.
